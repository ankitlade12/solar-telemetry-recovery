# Release status

The manuscript and experimental evidence are available in this local research workspace. No manuscript or research artifact has been submitted or published. Superseded distribution archives and copied staging trees have been removed from the working repository.

Local package builders remain available. Each new package must validate the current manuscript, evidence, dependency locks, and matching review records. Checks recorded for an older package do not certify a later revision.

Public distribution still requires selection of an original-code license and confirmation of copyright holders and author consent. Existing PVDAQ attribution and IEEEtran licensing apply to their respective materials; they do not supply a license for the original code.

The anonymous manuscript bundle and private research archive have different contents. The public dependency views omit the private planning document while retaining scientific dependencies and parent-lock identities. Packaging does not perform publication, submission, payment, or organizer contact.
