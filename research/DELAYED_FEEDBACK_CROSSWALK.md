# Delayed-feedback theory and the implemented control

Checked September 12, 2026. El Halabi and Brandt's [September 7 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07251v1) analyzes fixed-delay ACI. [Algorithm 2 and Sections 4.1–4.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.07251v1) update from the level saved with the issued interval, producing interleaved phase sequences. The long-run argument uses full/empty boundary prediction sets. Its approximate marginal analysis additionally assumes a stationary hidden-state model and regularity conditions. These sections were inspected; their proofs were not independently established here.

Our `SignedCalibrator.receive` scores the originally issued interval but applies its error to the **current** horizon-specific level, then clips to `[0.01, 0.99]`. Label arrivals can be irregular, duplicated, backfilled or permanently absent. Its score windows and repaired bounded intervals add further differences. It is therefore an empirical receipt-gated feedback control, not a reproduction of that fixed-delay recursion. The paper must not inherit its guarantee or claim that delayed conformal feedback itself is new. This clarification changes no frozen model or experiment.
