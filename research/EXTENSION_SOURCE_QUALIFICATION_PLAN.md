# Additional producing-site source qualification

This extension follows the post-exposure Colorado diagnostic. It does not amend the frozen 2017 experiment or replace its unfavorable results. No forecasting performance on the candidate sites is inspected during source qualification.

The initial candidates are PVDAQ 1367 (Henderson, Nevada) and 1239 (Presque Isle, Maine), selected from the existing metadata screen for geographic separation and cataloged AC/POA channels. Public object listings show daily objects for 2016 and 2017. System 10, near the original Colorado system, is a reserve engineering comparison; it is not an additional independent climate. Object presence alone does not qualify a dataset.

Before new forecasting, record each candidate's instrument/channel IDs, nameplate, units and scaling, actual sampling cadence, native/UTC timestamp availability, duplicate conflicts, missingness and monthly source distributions. Check positive near-idle power under substantial irradiance, not just exact zeros. Preserve raw values and all exclusions; do not silently substitute another channel for a more favorable result.

Initial screening uses cataloged aggregate AC and POA: system 1367 IDs 3091 and 4195; system 1239 IDs 3015 and 3018. The latter has a kW-looking sensor name, W units and a scale factor of 1000, requiring an explicit raw-versus-calculated value check before conversion. Minute, five-minute and fifteen-minute sources must not be treated as if they had the same per-hour sample requirement. Qualified hourly targets require every expected native sample and a justified interval convention.

For an initial producing-site screen, require usable paired AC/POA observations in every calendar month of both years, enough source support to form at least 70% of geometric-daylight hourly bins at a justified native cadence, and no unexplained dominant near-idle regime. A provisional diagnostic flag is AC below 1% of DC nameplate during POA above 200 W/m² in more than 10% of paired bright observations. These are source-screening choices before candidate measurements are inspected, not formal physical-fault labels or forecasting success gates. Borderline and failed cases remain documented; thresholds are not tuned against model performance.

Full timestamp and metrological qualification follows screening. If cadence, scale or channel semantics remain unresolved, retain a failed/pending record and do not describe that site as validated. Native missing outcomes remain missing. No measured weather from a future target interval can become a predictor.

Only after source qualification should a separate extension protocol select training/tuning periods, horizons, receipt schedules, fixed comparators, primary contrasts, support criteria and dependence-aware uncertainty. The original model configuration may be carried forward as a fixed replication; any new model selection requires development-only tuning. Additional-site 2017 forecasts would be a newly declared extension after exposure of the original study, not part of its original prospective freeze. No new superiority or generalization claim is made by acquiring data.
